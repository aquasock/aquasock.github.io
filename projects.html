01

Console + systems

The machines you run.

SHELL

TinyTang

The parent console shell: the TinyDesk desktop on HDMI, Bluetooth keyboards, mice and gamepads, storage, core loading, and diagnostics.

v0.2.1↗ NES

TinyNES

NES on FCEUmm on the AE350, with HDMI video, audio, wired Wii Classic and Bluetooth pads, and persistent save states.

Integrated→ SNES

TinySNES

nand2mario’s SNESTang ported to the Tang with the open flow, running in the FPGA fabric: retail cartridges including HiROM, DSP-1 and SuperFX titles, battery saves, pad remapping, and aspect and overscan settings.

Integrated↗ GBC

TinyGBC

Game Boy Color on gambatte on the AE350, with palettes, colour correction, battery saves, and cartridge clocks.

Integrated↗ GBA

TinyGBA

nand2mario’s GBATang ported to the Tang with the open flow, running in the FPGA fabric: retail cartridges with their save hardware set up at load and their saves kept on the card. Its TinyDesk window is on main since v0.2.1, not yet released.

Unreleased↗ T89

Tiny89

A TI-89 Titanium emulator on a TiEmu 3.04 core on the AE350, with the calculator’s LCD on HDMI and controller input. Needs your own OS image.

Integrated↗ AUD

TinyPhosphor

A music player and stereo XY visualiser: eleven codec families through Rockbox’s decoders on the AE350, the picture and I2S audio in the FPGA, built with the open flow.

Integrated↗

TinyCreature

TinyMaps

A map renderer and simulation path with dedicated HDMI and content-loading work.

An early investigation into bringing VCMI-oriented workloads to the Tiny ecosystem.
